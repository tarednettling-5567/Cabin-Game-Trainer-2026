## 🎮 About the Game

the cabin game is a chaotic first-person horror card game developed and published by Twisted Corridor. It entered Steam Early Access on August 27, 2026, and supports solo play and online co-op for one to four players. Players build and explore a changing cabin, uncover clues, survive monster encounters, and escape through card-driven progression.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **the cabin game**. This trainer adds optional quality-of-life and accessibility controls for the cabin game's card-based survival loop. It can pause or slow the shared clock, protect players during exploration and events, assist with card management, and reveal investigation information without adding combat systems that are not part of the game.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed solo experience, accessible co-op sessions, easier clue investigation, slower time pressure, or a convenient way to experiment with the game's card and cabin systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Freeze Shared Clock** — Pauses the cabin's shared time progression while players explore rooms, inspect clues, or coordinate their next card.
- **Slow Clock Progression** — Reduces the speed of the shared clock to give solo players and co-op groups more time to plan.

### 📌 Investigation

- **Reveal Clue Information** — Displays additional information for available clues and helps identify the room or evidence needed for the final escape.

### 📌 Cards

- **Unlimited Lifesaver Cards** — Prevents Lifesaver Cards from being consumed when used to push the approaching monster pawn backward.
- **Draw Assistance** — Improves access to useful cards during the table phase, including room, progress, clue, and event cards.

### 🌍 World

- **Safe Exploration** — Protects the player from ordinary failure while moving through the procedurally arranged cabin and examining its rooms.

### 📌 Survival

- **Event Protection** — Reduces or disables player elimination during timed horror events and monster encounters.
- **No Monster Pawn Advance** — Stops the monster pawn from advancing along the clock track during supported card effects.

### 📌 Progression

- **Unlimited Candy** — Adds candy for use in the Boo Factory, including character cosmetics, lobby pets, and tricks and treats.

### 🚶 Movement

- **Movement Assist** — Improves movement control during exploration and chase sequences without changing the cabin's room layout.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Conventional Combat** — The game has no standard weapon-based combat system. This trainer focuses on evasion, event survival, card play, clues, and escape progression.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement assist during exploration and chase events when the cabin's corridors become difficult to navigate. Keep the clock paused or slowed while learning room connections, then disable the assist for a standard challenge.

### Tools & Inventory

Card management takes place at the round table rather than through a traditional item inventory. Use draw assistance to experiment with room, progress, clue, and event cards. Unlimited Lifesaver Cards are useful when the monster pawn is close to catching the group.

### World & Environment

Room Cards determine how the cabin is built during a run, so the playable layout changes as the session progresses. Use clue reveal and safe exploration to inspect placed rooms, understand their connections, and prepare for the final escape.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no conventional combat with weapons or attack statistics. Threats are handled through card effects, movement, timed events, and survival decisions. Event protection and no monster pawn advance provide defensive assistance without introducing an unrelated combat mechanic.
